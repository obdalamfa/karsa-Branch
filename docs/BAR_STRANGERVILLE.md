# BAR_STRANGERVILLE.md — the mystery bar, measured

**What this is.** `_bench/BRIEF.md` §2 names two bars. Its sibling `docs/BAR_SIMS1.md`
covers the life-sim machine and its presentation. This one covers the other bar:
**The Sims 4: StrangerVille (2019) as the reference for mystery *structure* and
*escalation* — explicitly not for art style.** Nothing here asks for a red mesa, a
Joshua tree, a grey alien decal or hazmat yellow. Lembah Karsa is an Indonesian folk
village and its entity speaks only the vocabulary in `docs/ENTITY_VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md`.
What we are stealing is the *machine* underneath StrangerVille: how a slow horror is
staged inside a life sim that never stops running.

**Sources.** The seven EA-published screenshots in `_bench/refs/strangerville/`
(Steam appid 1235742; provenance in that folder's `SOURCE.txt`), read directly; plus
shipped-story documentation fetched and read for this document — carls-sims-4-guide.com's
Acts 1–4, Story Summary (transcribed from the in-game archives and dialogue), Town Guide and
Cheats pages; sims.fandom.com's *Possessed*, *Mother Plant*, *StrangerVille* and pack
articles; and simscommunity.info's full story walkthrough and aspiration overview.
Facts taken from those are stated plainly; **everything that is our invention is
marked [delta].**

restored Victorian houses with white picket fences, Joshua trees. Foreground left: a

like a town.

### Synthesis — five staging rules extracted from the frames

1. The ordinary and the wrong share the frame, at full daylight, unretouched.
2. The infected are normal people with a changed face and a changed idle.
3. Mystery UI is the life sim's own UI: thought balloons, inventory, notebook, catalogue
   cards.
4. Atmosphere is objects with discrete states, not a shader over everything.
5. The town commercialises its own rumour, and the entity's icon is already merch.

## 1. The staging principle

> **Simulasi hidup tidak pernah berhenti.**
> *The life sim never pauses for the mystery.*

A hard constraint on every slice, not a mood note. As rules a builder can be failed
against:

**S1. No modal horror.** The mystery never takes exclusive control of the frame, the
clock, the camera or the input. Every ordinary affordance available at stage 0 is
available at stage 5, including during the final confrontation.

**S2. Needs keep decaying.** `lapar` / `sosial` / `senang` keep ticking through every
mystery beat. A Sim can get hungry in the middle of the confrontation and go eat. The
motive thermometers in the blue panel never freeze and never hide.

**S3. Schedules keep executing.** `npc_brain` / `behavior_vm` keep running routines at
every stage. Shops keep their hours, the harvest timer keeps counting, the market NPC
still sells *pisang goreng* under the manifestation.

**S4. No sting, no riser, no grade.** No jump-scare audio, no vignette, no global
desaturation, no letterbox, no camera takeover, no slow motion. `game/sound.py` keeps
village ambience at **full level** at every stage; the entity's audio is a bed
*underneath* it, never a replacement.

**S5. Every horror asset is a catalogue object** — a price, a footprint, a buy-mode card;
placeable, movable and sellable like a chair. The bar takes this to its limit: **the Mother
Plant herself is a buy-mode object**, reachable with `bb.showhiddenobjects`, with her root as
a separate placeable piece. The final boss is furniture.

**S6. The presentation never announces the beat.** The player finds out something is a
horror beat by *noticing*, not by being cued. (Corollary from
`ENTITY_VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md` §3 stage 4: *never break a control the player needs* — the
clock keeps telling the true time even while its rivets turn bronze.)

### The bar's own proof that it means this

StrangerVille's possession is not a cutscene state, it is **an emotion**. Per fandom's
*Possessed* article the moodlet dumps seven-million-plus points into the possessed emotion
so it outranks every other mood — but it is delivered through the same emotion system that
handles Flirty and Bored. A possessed Sim keeps walking, keeps sitting, keeps being talked
to, and keeps accruing relationship points with whoever talks to them. EA did not build a
horror mode; **they wrote one more mood into the mood system.**

The whole infection decomposes into three pieces of pre-existing life-sim plumbing and
nothing else: a **trait** (`Infected`, permanent, cheatable with `Traits.Equip_Trait`, like
Gloomy or Slob), a **moodlet** on a timer, and an **emotion** the moodlet feeds. The reward
for finishing is likewise a trait (`HeroOfStrangerVille`). There is no fourth system. Every
piece of the horror is a row in a table the game already had — which is exactly why the town
around it never has to stop.

The one place the sim machinery does bend is instructive: a possessed Sim's **motives lock
at full**. The horror is not that their needs suffer, it is that their needs stop
mattering — the clearest possible statement inside a needs-driven game that something else
is driving now. We take that idea and the restraint that comes with it.

### The failure mode, named

> **"Mode Horor"** — *the horror mode.*
> The moment the game enters a special state where ordinary interactions are suspended,
> the camera is taken away, the music swaps to a horror bed, and the player's only verbs
> are mystery verbs. **A horror game where the life sim pauses is not StrangerVille.** It
> is a different genre wearing StrangerVille's assets, and every blind judge will pick the
> bar over us.

Three secondary failure modes, all Mode Horor in disguise:

- **Kota Kosong (the empty town).** NPC density silently drops as the stage rises, so late
  frames have nobody ordinary in them. If the town empties, the contrast dies and the
  horror has nothing to be measured against.
- **Gradasi (the grade).** A global colour grade, fog or bloom doing work that geometry and
  object state should do. Test: photograph stage 0 and stage 3 from an identical camera; if
  the difference survives greyscale + histogram equalisation it was real; if it vanishes,
  it was a grade.
- **Sinematik (the cutscene).** Any moment where input is taken away for longer than one
  interaction's animation.

### The two acceptance tests for the principle

- **T-ORDINARY:** at *every* stage the player can complete a full chore loop
  (tanam → siram → panen → jual) with zero mystery interruption, and that loop is
  photographable end to end.
- **T-COEXIST:** at every stage ≥ 2 there exists a camera from which one frame contains
  both a mystery element and an unrelated NPC completing an ordinary interaction.

## 2. The stage machine

### 2.1 What StrangerVille actually runs

The story is **a single world-scoped narrative variable**, not a per-Sim quest flag. The
debug console reaches it directly:

```
narrative.start_narrative narrative_strangetown_act1
narrative.start_narrative narrative_strangetown_act2
narrative.start_narrative narrative_strangetown_act3
narrative.start_narrative narrative_strangetown_act4   # resolved; everyone normal
secret_lab.reveal_next_section / reveal_all_sections / reset_lab
```

Four structural facts fall out of that:

1. **The world holds the state, not the player.** Every household sees the same act. Move a
   family in after the story is solved and they arrive in a solved town.
2. **Advancement is caused by player action, never by a timer.** Confirmed by the pack's own
   answer to "what if I stop investigating?" — nothing happens; the story simply does not
   progress, and you can live in StrangerVille for years without touching it. The entry
   trigger is not a quest-giver either: the story opens when the player uses the ordinary
   *Talk About StrangerVille* social, or interacts with a bizarre fruit. **The mystery is
   entered through the friendship menu.** The four aspiration milestones are then exactly
   three objectives each — **New Resident** (talk about StrangerVille with 5 Sims · buy a
   curio from the Curio Shop · examine the sealed door at the Secret Lab) → **Personal Spy**
   (gather 15 pieces of evidence · compile a Secret Dossier · open the sealed door) →
   **Pathogenic Investigator** (collect 15 spore clusters · assemble the hazmat suit ·
   traverse to the deepest part of the Secret Lab) → **StrangerVille Hero** (vaccinate
   3 Sims · recruit 3 Sims to help stop the source · defeat the source of the infection).
   Note what is *not* a milestone: constructing the Infection Profile Data, obtaining the
   spore filter, obtaining the scanner and building the vaccines are all mandatory work that
   the checklist never names. **The visible list is coarser than the actual play.** That gap
   is deliberate and we copy it: an objective line points at an outcome, and the player works
   out the route.
3. **The escalation is the player's fault.** Opening the lab door starts **The Bloom**:
   *"Leaving the Secret Lab you can't help but notice the sky has shifted to an eerie
   color… Opening that lab door must have released some kind of toxin into the air."* The
   bizarre plants double in size. **The investigation is the thing that feeds the
   antagonist.** That is the moral engine and we are stealing it whole.
4. **The space reveals itself in sections.** `reveal_next_section` — the lab is not one room
   that unlocks but a stack of them, gated first by keycard, then by hazmat suit.

Per-act world deltas as shipped:

| Act | Trigger | Sky / weather | Plants | Sims | Military | Player can |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | move in | ordinary desert sun | bizarre plants scattered, small | some possessed; jerky gait, contorted faces, manic laughter; spawn on public lots **at night**, readily on residential lots any hour | drink at the bar; vans patrol scanning for infection; raid homes and **confiscate an infected fridge** (reimbursed) | talk, photograph plants, search piles, hack, bug, buy curios, examine the sealed door |
| 2 | open the lab door with a keycard | **The Bloom** — sky shifts to an eerie colour; townsfolk remark on the cloud building over the crater | all bizarre plants **double in size** | possessed now spawn on public lots **at any hour** | now talk about scanning for spores | scan for spore clusters, make Infection Profile Data, get the spore filter, assemble hazmat |
| 3 | traverse to the deepest lab | air worsens by the hour; **a massive storm rages over the crater**; the world's named "Strange Weather" type is live | fully bloomed everywhere | town on high alert, nerves frayed | — | make vaccines, test on 3 possessed, vaccinate and recruit 3 allies |
| 4 | defeat the Mother | returns to normal | subside | the infected wake; the Infected trait is stripped from **every Sim in the world**, even outside StrangerVille | — | **set the strangeness level at will**; revive the Mother with a bizarre fruit and replay |

Act 4 is the part most designers miss: the resolution is not a reset, it is **a dial handed
to the player**. Revived with a bizarre fruit, the Mother becomes a relationship target who
grants one wish per day (money, skills, lifespan) and will re-infect the town on request —
and requesting strangeness after the cure generates a *fresh* set of possessed townies.
Carl's own summary: *"The Hero of Strangerville manifests itself as an entirely new problem
the town should fear."*

### 2.2 The infection as a state machine on the Sim

Worth its own table, because it is the most transferable thing in the pack — two infection
routes with completely different tempos:

| Route | Duration | After-effect | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walk into a toxic spore cloud (lab level 2) | possessed **30 minutes**, then normal | motives max out | strictly *beneficial* — a free need-fill for a scare |
| Eat a bizarre fruit, or drink bizarre fruit juice | possessed **3 hours** | permanent **Infected** trait until vaccinated; possessed again **every 24 h starting at 1 a.m.** | juice while already infected → 5 hours |

While possessed: player control is limited and interactions are overridden with wandering
and staring at objects; the Sim's neck cranes to **90°** with limbs doll-stretched and a
fixed wide smile; motives lock at full; replies to conversation are single cryptic lines
about "the Mother"; and when the possessed Sim starts the conversation the usual
interaction bar **does not appear** at the top of the screen. The socials it *is* given are
ordinary-shaped and wrong — documented as talking about "a Normal Sim's problems", about
flowers, and about "the Mother", plus a greeting *Welcome to StrangerVille* that a possessed
Sim will use even when standing in a completely different world. That greeting is also a
functional introduction: it satisfies the game's normal "attempt to introduce" gate. **The
possessed use the friendship system correctly.** They are not outside the social machine,
they are inside it, saying the wrong thing.

And there is a counter-play we must not lose: **another Sim can slap them awake**, and it
works only if the relationship is high enough — otherwise the possessed Sim catches the
hand and bends it backwards, and even a good friend may need several attempts. A social
system used as a rescue verb, gated on a number the player spent the whole game building.
That is the best single mechanic in the pack.

### 2.3 Our machine

We already have the ladder: `docs/ENTITY_VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md` §3 defines five stages and
`game/entity_style.py` already ships `STAGE_BUDGET` and `check_stage_budget()`. **This
document does not invent a second ladder.** It supplies the state machine that drives the
one that exists.

State lives in **`game/state.py` → `entity_stage: int = 0`** (world-scoped, serialised with
the save, exactly like StrangerVille's narrative variable), driven from
**`game/controllers/quest_controller.py`** beside the existing `quest_stage`.
`tools/capture.py` gains `--taint N` so every state is photographable.

| # | Name | Trigger (player action only) | NPC behaviour | Ambient audio | Sky / weather | Object state | UI | Player can |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| **1** | `SALAH SUDUT` | first ordinary chore completed on a tainted tile (harvest there / draw from the well) | unchanged | unchanged | unchanged | ≤3 tainted props, <0.15 % of screen | none | ordinary game; nothing signals |

| **R** | `TENANG` | resolve the confrontation | the cured wake; schedules restore | entity layer fades over 3 in-game days, never to zero | back to `state.weather` | tainted objects decay to level 0 over days, **not instantly** | BERKAS stays, now readable as a finished record | **set `entity_stage` at will from a diegetic object** |

Rules on the machine itself:

- **M1. Player-caused only.** No transition fires on a timer, a day count, or scene entry.
  Every arrow is something the player chose. The escalation must feel like *their* fault,
  because it is.
- **M2. Monotonic forward, deliberate backward.** `entity_stage` never decreases except
  through the `TENANG` dial. No silent regression on reload.
- **M3. One notification, diegetic, Indonesian, via `panels.flash_msg`.** No full-screen
  card. Stage 3 example: *"Langit di atas lembah berubah warna. Pintu itu tidak seharusnya
  kau buka."* One line, 3.5 s, over an unpaused game.
- **M4. Stage gates content, never controls.** Nothing available at stage 0 becomes
  unavailable at stage 5.
- **M5. Budgets are enforced in code** via `entity_style.check_stage_budget()`. A slice that
  exceeds its budget fails regardless of how good the frame looks.
- **M6. Persisted and photographable.** `entity_stage` round-trips through save/load, and
  `--taint N` forces it before warmup frames.
- **M7. Possession is a mood, not a mode [delta].** It is written into the existing
  needs/mood path in `state.py`, it locks the three motives at full while it lasts, and it is
  escapable by *tampar* — gated on `state.npc_hearts`, failing with a caught wrist at low
  affection. Two acquisition routes with different tempos, exactly as the bar has them: a
  *hawa* cloud (short, ends clean, motives filled) and *Buah Ganjil* (permanent taint,
  recurring nightly at a fixed hour).

## 3. The clue economy

### 3.1 What StrangerVille actually runs

Three currencies, each with a different acquisition verb.

**Currency 1 — Evidence ×15 → the Dossier.** Sources, all optional, all redundant:

| Source | Verb | Cost / gate |
|---|---|---|
| Junk piles and folder piles at the lab and inside residents' homes | Search | free, spatial |
| Bizarre plants around town, and the sealed door itself | Photograph | a camera |
| Lab computers | Hack | Programming 3 — and **Programming 10** decrypts a USB drive that yields *a whole dossier in one go* |
| Any Sim, anywhere | Talk About StrangerVille | social time |
| Scientists (library) / Military (bar) | Ask for evidence | friendship |
| **Scientists, Military and Conspiracy Theorists only** | Plant a bug, then Listen | Listening Device §500 + bugs §25; yields either a Top Secret Recording *or* blackmail material. Bugging an ordinary townie produces nothing — the surveillance verb is gated on *who is worth watching*, which is itself a clue |
| Library archives | Read | free — **and entirely missable; this is where the actual plot lives** |

Compile fifteen with one click on the stack in your inventory. The Dossier then opens
**several independent doors** to the keycard: buy it from the Curio Shop's Secret Inventory
(§250), convince a Scientist, or convince a Military Sim — and if either refuses, the fix is
not a different item, it is *more friendship*. Two further routes bypass the dossier
entirely: a Sim in the **Military career** can *Requisition a Keycard* from a computer, and
players report that uploading the secret files to a hacker forum from any laptop gets a card
**mailed to you the next day**. Note the shape of that last one: a door opened by the
Programming skill, at home, on furniture the base game already had.

**Currency 2 — Spore Clusters ×15, gathered with a charged tool.** The Infection Scanner
must first be extracted from a Military Sim at the bar by *bribe (§750) / fight / impress
with Fitness / seduce*. It gives **three readings per battery charge**, each successful
reading yielding **up to five clusters**; a reading on thin air **burns the use and returns
nothing**, and a drained battery takes several in-game hours to recover. Fifteen clusters at
the Chemical Analyzer in the lab basement become the Infection Profile Data; a Scientist at
the library, at roughly 30–40 % friendship, will then have a **Spore Filter mailed to you
the next day**; the hazmat suit itself is §1,000 from the Secret Inventory, and you click
one on the other in your inventory to combine them.

**Currency 3 — Bizarre Fruit.** *Nurture* a strange plant; one fruit per plant, so three
fruit means walking to three plants (or §9 each at the Curio Shop). Each vaccine costs
**5 clusters + 1 fruit**, and the story wants six or seven vaccines: three to test on
possessed townies, then real ones for yourself and three allies.

### 3.2 Why it is not a checklist

Six properties, and these are the ones we must reproduce:

1. **Redundancy.** No single source is required. Two players' fifteen pieces are different
   fifteen pieces.
2. **The life sim is the key ring.** Skill, friendship, money, career rank, charisma and
   fitness are *alternative keys to the same door*. There is no bolted-on detective
   subsystem; the mystery is solved with the systems the game already had.
3. **Scarcity with a spatial rule.** Three readings per charge converts "gather 15" into
   "work out where the density is" — a map problem, not a counter.
4. **Losable and re-earnable.** Evidence is an inventory item. Players really do sell it by
   accident; the documented answer is "go and get more", not "reload".
5. **The meaning is off the critical path.** The archives explain the crater, the military's
   arrival and the Incident, and you can finish the whole story without reading one word.
   The *plot* is optional; only the *actions* are mandatory.
6. **Social cost.** Half the sources cost you standing with somebody: bugging, blackmail,
   fighting a soldier for his scanner.

| 1 | Kotak pos / surat | Baca | `state.mail_read`, `data.LORE_ITEMS` | free |

Rules **[delta]**:

- **C1.** No single source may supply more than **5 of 15**. Enforced in code.
- **C2.** At least **two** sources must be completable with **zero simoleons**.
- **C3.** Every Bukti is a real inventory item with an icon, one line of Indonesian text and
  a `sumber` (provenance) field. It can be sold. That is a trap and it stays a trap.
- **C4.** Compiling is one click on the stack, exactly as the bar does it.
- **C5.** At least **three** independent routes past the sealed door: buy the token from
  *Barang Simpanan*, convince a villager at high `npc_hearts`, or craft it.
- **C6.** The archives path must be able to explain the entire plot and must never be
  required by any objective.

**Serbuk ×15, via the Pengukur Hawa** — our scanner **[delta]**. A brass-and-glass incense
instrument obtained from a villager by *bayar / adu / bujuk / pamer* (four routes, same
shape as the soldier's scanner). **Three readings per charge**, five Serbuk on a hit, **a
miss consumes the reading**, and the charge returns on the world clock (about six in-game
hours). Density is a real per-tile field written by the hawa cloud objects — which, per
`strangerville_05.jpg`, exist at **three discrete densities as placeable objects**, not as a
shader.

**Buah Ganjil ×3** — *Rawat* a tainted crop, one fruit per plant, so three fruit is three
walks. This reuses the existing crop/harvest path, which makes the farming economy literally
the horror economy. And — straight from the bar — **eating one infects you**: the lure is a
fruit that looks good.

**Anti-checklist enforcement.** A slice fails the clue economy if: the panel ever displays a
"next step" instruction; a counter is the only feedback for progress; one source can be
farmed to 15; any source has no cost at all (time, money, standing or distance); or the
story can be completed without touching at least four distinct sources.

## 4. The investigation UI

### 4.1 What StrangerVille actually shows

Nothing bespoke, and this is the surprise: the pack ships **no detective screen**. Six
existing surfaces carry the whole mystery.

- **The Notebook** (opened from the phone) — where clues and story progress are recorded as
  they are found. It is the base game's own notebook, the same one that logs fishing and
  gardening finds. The mystery gets a section in it; it does not get an app.
- **The aspiration panel** — four milestones × three-ish objectives, a plain visible
  checklist.
- **The inventory** — evidence pieces, the Dossier, the scanner with its charge state, the
  keycard, the suit, the vaccines. The mystery is *things you carry*.
- **Thought balloons** — `_00` grinning green face, `_01` polaroid, `_04` "?" and
  footprints-under-magnifier. Moment-to-moment commentary in one object icon.
- **Notification toasts and phone messages** — the story's prose, one line at a time. Note
  that after meeting a possessed Sim you may get a **text message from them** reading like
  `JOIN US IN THE CRATER`. The antagonist already writes into the player's own channels.
- **The relationship panel** — after the fight, the Mother appears in it as a contactable
  Sim whose friendship bar starts deep in the negatives. The antagonist is a row in the same
  list as your neighbours.
- **The map, with a hole censored into it.** The lab's lot is labelled **`The ██████ Lab`** —
  a redacted proper noun sitting permanently in the world-selection UI, in the same type as
  "8 Bells" and "StrangerVille Information Center". It is the cheapest and best mystery
  device in the pack: a piece of chrome that admits, every time the player opens the map,
  that something is being kept from them. Players finish the story and come back asking how
  to un-redact it. You cannot.

Only the final fight gets bespoke HUD: the player's **infection meter** (must not fill)
against **the Mother's health** (must reach zero), with click-to-command on allies and on
the Mother — *Throw Infection Vaccine*, *Mega Spray*, *Retreat*, *Be Careful*, *Charge!*,
*Take a Breather*, *Warbling War Cry*. Possessed townies wander into the room mid-fight and
tie your allies up. Attempt it alone and the Mother eats your Sim; lose it and your allies
are possessed.

Note what is **not** shown anywhere: there is no screen that tells you who is infected. You
read that off the body, in the world.

**One documented absence, because the brief asked.** StrangerVille has **no rumour
bulletin board.** The pack ships corkboards and notice-board decor as furniture, but town
rumour is carried entirely by the *Talk About StrangerVille* social, the Curio vendor's
patter, possessed Sims' cryptic one-liners, notification toasts, phone messages and the
library archives. Our *papan pengumuman balai desa* is therefore **[delta]** — a place where
`MENANAM_RUMOR` (§5, mechanic 6) can be seen accumulating in writing, and one of the few
places where a manipulation leaves a paper record the player can photograph.

### 4.2 Ours **[delta]**

**A fifth tab on the blue Sims-1 control panel: `BERKAS`**, opened by
`panels.open_panel('berkas')` beside Live / Buy / Build. It appears at stage 2 and never
leaves. Four regions:

1. **Papan bukti** — a 5×3 corkboard of the fifteen Bukti slots. Collected items show their
   icon; uncollected show a grey silhouette of the *shape* of that evidence class. Hover
   gives its line of text and its `sumber`.
2. **Yang kau tahu** — eight one-sentence claims about the valley, each carrying a state:
   `PASTI` (confirmed) / `KABAR` (rumour) / `BERTENTANGAN` (contradicted). Derived from the
   Bukti set, never hand-authored. **This list is the antagonist's primary attack surface**
   (§5).
3. **Peta** — the village map, pinned where evidence was found and where warga were
   *terakhir dilihat*.
4. **Warga** — a strip of small portraits with a last-seen note and a one-word posture read.
   Never a badge saying "infected". The player decides.

Rules:

- **U1.** The panel never opens itself and never shows a "next step".
- **U2.** It is a *record*, not a tracker: it answers "what do I know", never "what do I do".
- **U3.** It is diegetic — your Sim's notebook, following the bar's own choice to put clues
  in the Notebook rather than in a HUD. Buying the notebook creates the tab; losing the
  notebook loses the tab. (And the mob knows this.)
- **U4.** Thought balloons carry the moment-to-moment with exactly three mystery icons: `?`
  (tanda tanya), jejak-kaki-di-kaca (investigation), mata-dalam-daun (the entity noticed
  you). One object icon per balloon, Sims-1 style.
- **U5.** The confrontation HUD is a *strip added to the existing panel*, never a
  replacement. Clock, simoleons and motive thermometers stay on screen and stay live for the
  whole fight.
- **U6.** No screen ever labels a villager as infected.
- **U7.** The mob may write into any surface listed above **except** the clock, the money
  counter and the motive thermometers. Those three are the player's instruments and stay
  honest — see A3.
- **U8. One permanent redaction.** Exactly one place name in the valley renders censored on
  the map and in the *Peta* region — *`Candi ███████`* — from the first frame of a new game
  to after the resolution. It is never revealed, by any means, ever. Copied wholesale from
  `The ██████ Lab`: a standing admission in the chrome that the world is keeping something,
  costing one string and no systems.

## 5. The manipulating antagonist

### 5.1 The gap

StrangerVille's Mother is, mechanically, **a stationary boss with a global status aura**.
She emits a condition; the town degrades on a schedule the player triggers; she waits at the
bottom of a hole with a health bar. In every beat the antagonist is *a substance* — spores.
Nobody in StrangerVille ever suspects **a person** of authoring the mystery, because no
person is.

The bar does gesture at more, twice, and both gestures are our seed: possessed Sims **text
you cryptic messages**, and the defeated Mother becomes **a relationship row who sets the
town's strangeness on request**. Both moments are the antagonist reaching into a system the
player uses to understand the world. StrangerVille does it twice, as flavour.
`_bench/BRIEF.md` §1 demands it as the spine: an entity **imitating life** whose principal
mob is **manipulating the story from inside it**. So:

> **An NPC steers the narrative when it has write access to the same data structures the
> player reads to understand the world.**

The player reads: clue items, dialogue, NPC positions and routines, the lore list, the quest
log, relationship records, the map, the panel. An antagonist that merely *fights* touches
none of them. Ours writes to all of them.

Three constraints on all eight mechanics, without which this is just a bug:

- **A1. Falsifiable by ordinary means.** Every manipulation must be detectable with tools the
  player already has — a photograph, a copy in a chest, a second witness, the clock, a
  written note. Manipulation the player cannot in principle catch is indistinguishable from a
  defect.
- **A2. Leaves a physical trace.** Every edit drops one object or one motion obeying
  `ENTITY_VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md` §4 **ORTHOGONALITY INTRUSION** — a bronze rivet, an exact 90°,
  a snapped 0.25 m length, a verbatim repetition. (The bar itself hands us the vocabulary:
  the possessed neck cranes to exactly 90°.)
- **A3. Never breaks a control.** The panel keeps working, the clock keeps telling the true
  time, the save keeps saving. The mob edits *content*, never *function*.

Write-access ladder: stage 2 → nothing; stage 3 → mechanics 1 and 6; stage 4 → + 2, 3, 7;
stage 5 → + 4, 5, 8. **At most two manipulation events live at once** before stage 5.

### 5.2 Eight mechanics

**1. `BUKTI_PALSU` — the forged clue.**
The mob writes a fabricated Bukti into a container the player has not yet searched. It is
pixel-identical to a real one; only its `sumber` hash matches no real site. Compiled into the
Berkas it pushes one of the eight *Yang kau tahu* claims to a wrong `PASTI`.
*Observable:* two evidence items with the same icon and mutually contradicting text in one
inventory; later the claim flips to `BERTENTANGAN` with a struck-through line.
*Counter:* compare `sumber` fields; or find the real item at the real site.

**2. `JADWAL_DITULIS_ULANG` — the schedule rewrite.**
The mob writes directly into `npc_brain` / `behavior_vm` schedule slots. The three villagers
who could corroborate the player's current claim get overtime, an errand, or a sudden shared
destination — and are simply *not where they said they would be* on the night the player
needs them.
*Observable:* an NPC states a routine in dialogue (*"aku selalu di pasar tiap sore"*) and is
absent three days running. The tell is that their new destinations are **geometric** — three
paths meeting at an exact right angle — where genuine scheduling scatters.
*Counter:* follow one; read the map pins.

**3. `MUNCUL_DI_INGATAN` — it appears in memories.**
The mob rewrites already-collected lore. A note read on day 3 gains a sentence; a described
scene gains a figure standing at the back of it.
*Observable:* the Berkas entry shows *"catatan ini berubah"* with a count.
*Counter:* **a copy stored in a chest is immune** — the mob reaches only items in the Sim's
own inventory. This teaches hoarding, and hoarding is the correct instinct.

**4. `SUNTING_QUEST_LOG` — it edits the quest log.**
A `QUEST_STAGES` objective's Indonesian text changes between two openings of the panel. The
objective still completes correctly (A3), but it now names the wrong person or the wrong
place.
*Observable:* the objective reads differently than it did, and the panel's footer honestly
logs *"tujuan ini berubah 2×"*. The mob can rewrite the goal; it cannot suppress the edit
counter. *Counter:* screenshot, or the counter itself.

**5. `MENYAMAR` — it wears a villager.**
For exactly one conversation the mob assumes an existing villager's actor id, model and
dialogue table. A perfect copy but for one orthogonality intrusion — head yaw snapping to
exactly 90° and holding 1.2 s, or an arm swing with the fillet removed.
*Observable:* **the real villager is somewhere else at the same in-game minute**, and both
can be photographed. *Counter:* two frames at one timestamp; or catching the snap.

**6. `MENANAM_RUMOR` — it seeds a rumour.**
The mob injects one sentence into the town's shared gossip pool, and pins it to the *papan
pengumuman*. Within two in-game days three unrelated villagers speak it **verbatim**.
*Observable:* verbatim repetition. People in this codebase paraphrase — `data.py` dialogue
rotates through `npc_dialog_index`. The entity copies exactly, and exact copying is its
signature everywhere else in the game.
*Counter:* ask three people about the same subject; or compare the board to your own notes.

**7. `MEMINDAH_BUKTI` — it moves evidence.**
The mob removes an item from a container the player has opened but not emptied, or takes one
from the player's own inventory during sleep, leaving a bronze rivet in the slot.
*Observable:* the inventory count drops; a `RIVET` object sits in the chest; the ordinary
item-pickup sound plays **at 3 a.m.** with nobody on the lot.
*Counter:* lock it in a chest; or stay awake.

**8. `MENGARANG_SEKUTU` — it fabricates an ally.**
The mob spawns a helpful villager who is not in the town roster: full portrait, full
relationship bar, genuinely useful clues — all of which point one way, toward advancing
`entity_stage` early, which serves the mob.
*Observable:* the *Warga* strip shows their slot blank or duplicating another villager's
portrait; no house on the map is theirs; no other NPC holds a relationship value with them.
*Counter:* the roster; or asking a third villager about them and getting a blank.

### 5.3 What this buys us over the bar

StrangerVille's player asks *"what happened here?"*. Ours must be able to ask **"is what I
know still mine?"** — and must be able to *answer* it, with a photograph, a locked chest, and
a friend who was standing there too. That is the delta, and it is the whole reason the entity
in `docs/entity-logo.svg` has eyes on its leaves and a circuit board where its roots should
be.

## 6. Acceptance checklist — 25 observables

Format follows the Sims 1 bar: one line per observable, each photographable from the real
game via `tools/capture.py`, each tagged with what it takes to photograph it —

- **single** — one frame settles it.
- **pair** — two frames that must be compared (identical camera, or two timestamps).
- **sequence** — three or more frames read in order; a single frame cannot prove it.

Counts: **6 single, 5 pair, 14 sequence.** All captures at 1920×1080 per `BRIEF.md` §4.
**Fourteen of the twenty-five cannot be settled by one frame** and need a capture sequence,
because what is being judged is a change over time — a chore completing while the world is
wrong, a stage advancing, a scanner charge being spent, a claim flipping, a promise being
broken across days. A further five need a *pair*: two frames from one identical camera
differing only in the named state. Only six are single-frame observables, and none of the
six is about behaviour. **That ratio is the point of this document.** A single frame can
prove a look; it cannot prove that the life sim kept running. The fourteen are listed again
in §6.1 so the composer can plan the runs.

**Staging principle**

- [ ] **SV-01** — At `--taint 3`, a full chore loop (tanam → siram → panen → jual) completes
  with zero mystery interruption and the motive thermometers move throughout.

  state, not by a colour grade: the difference survives greyscale + histogram equalisation.

## 7. What `tools/capture.py` must gain

Per `BRIEF.md` §4 — *a feature a critic cannot photograph does not exist* — most rows above
are unreachable with today's flags (`--out --scene --frames --hour --keys --at --pitch
--yaw --dist --dump --width --height`). Required additions:

| Flag | Purpose | Needed by |
|---|---|---|
| `--taint N` | force `state.entity_stage` (0–5) before warmup | SV-01, 02, 05, 09, 12, 15 |
| `--story N` | force quest/clue progress independently of taint | SV-16 – SV-20 |

| `--pair a,b` | two captures from one identical camera differing only in the named state | SV-02, 12, 20, 23, 24 |
| `--dump-story` | print `entity_stage`, Bukti count by `sumber`, live manipulation events | SV-14, 16, 22 |

Until those land the corresponding rows cannot be ticked, and a slice that cannot be
photographed is not done.
